Invoice Tracker

Track each case from invoice issued → customer payment → supplier cash → client payout. Partial payments supported.
⚠️ This preview window cannot save data permanently — your entries will disappear when you close it. To keep your records, open invoice-tracker.html by double-clicking it in Finder (it opens in Safari/Chrome), or click Backup before closing to download your data.
💡 How to update a case: click the Update button (or anywhere on the row) to open the case — there you tick Invoice Issued and key in every payment received from the customer, cash from the supplier, and cash paid to the client (partial amounts are fine). Use Edit to change the company/amount/%, and Del to delete the case. You can also click the Yes/No under Invoice to flip it directly.
Data is saved automatically in this browser. Use Backup regularly to save a copy of your data as a file, and Restore to load it back.
